# Summary Report: “AWS Agentic AI Build Week Technical Sharing Sessions”

### Event Objectives

- Share practical experiences from AWS Agentic AI Build Week projects
- Demonstrate how AI-native applications are designed and deployed on AWS
- Introduce modern cloud architecture and Agentic AI development practices
- Present real-world applications built using AWS AI and serverless services

### Speakers

- Members of **Team 3KA** – *Hackathon Journey*
- Members of **Plan V** – *Solution Architect Professional Native App*
- Members of **OneTeam** – *Colonel AI*
- Members of **SignalScout Team**

### Key Highlights

#### Hackathon Journey

The speakers shared their experience participating in the AWS Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon, from forming a team and validating ideas to building an MVP within 24 hours. They emphasized teamwork, rapid prototyping, and learning through hands-on development rather than focusing only on the competition results.

#### AI-powered Crowd Monitoring System

The **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D** project demonstrated how computer vision, Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, and a React dashboard were combined to monitor crowd density, predict congestion, and provide proactive recommendations for operators. The presentation highlighted the importance of scalable AI systems and explainable decision-making.

#### AI-native Solution Architect

The speakers introduced an AI-powered solution capable of analyzing project requirements, generating AWS architectures with official AWS Architecture Icons, producing editable Draw.io diagrams, estimating AWS costs, and refining system designs through an interactive chat interface.

#### Agentic AI for Business Applications

The **Colonel AI** project demonstrated how Agentic AI can automate conversational ordering by understanding user intent, interacting with business tools, validating orders, and supporting multiple communication channels through a scalable microservices architecture.

#### Enterprise AI Decision Support

The **SignalScout** project showcased how AI can collect, analyze, and connect enterprise information from multiple sources to identify strategic business signals, assess risks, and support decision-making using evidence-based insights.

### Key Takeaways

#### AI-native Application Development

- Build practical AI applications by combining foundation models with cloud services.
- Design systems that are scalable, maintainable, and easy to extend.
- Use AI agents to automate business workflows while keeping humans involved in important decisions.

#### Cloud Architecture

- Apply serverless and microservices architectures to improve scalability.
- Design loosely coupled systems using modern AWS services.
- Balance performance, cost, and maintainability throughout the system lifecycle.

#### Software Engineering Practices

- Build a minimum viable product (MVP) before adding advanced features.
- Work effectively under time constraints through collaboration and clear task allocation.
- Continuously improve solutions using feedback and iterative development.

### Applying to Work

- Apply modular architecture principles when designing cloud applications.
- Consider scalability and cost optimization during system implementation.
- Explore AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity.
- Design workflows that combine automation with human decision-making.
- Adopt rapid prototyping and iterative development practices in future projects.

### Event Experience

Participating in these technical sharing sessions provided valuable insights into how modern AI applications are designed, implemented, and deployed on AWS. Instead of focusing only on theoretical concepts, the speakers shared practical experiences, architectural decisions, technical challenges, and lessons learned while developing real-world projects.

#### Learning from Real-world Projects

- Learned how different teams designed AI-native solutions to solve practical business problems.
- Understood how AWS AI services, serverless technologies, and modern software architectures work together in production-ready systems.

#### Practical Technical Knowledge

- Explored Agentic AI, cloud-native architectures, computer vision, and conversational AI applications.
- Learned how to design systems that balance scalability, maintainability, and operational cost.
- Observed how experienced teams approached rapid prototyping, MVP development, and architecture validation.

#### Inspiration for Future Development

- The sessions demonstrated that successful AI projects require not only technical knowledge but also effective teamwork, communication, and continuous learning.
- The speakers openly shared both their successes and challenges, providing practical guidance for students and future software engineers.
